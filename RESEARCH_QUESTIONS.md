# Research Questions

The project was organized around questions rather than around maximizing a backtest.

## Core questions

1. Does the strategy remain profitable over long historical periods?
2. How sensitive is performance to transaction costs?
3. Does performance vary by market regime?
4. Are recent results statistically different from older results?
5. Do losing or winning streaks contain useful conditional information?
6. Are sequence patterns stable through time?
7. Does performance vary by season, month, weekday, or hour?
8. Are long and short trades equally strong?
9. How does risk-per-trade affect drawdown and account survival?
10. Can the strategy be automated without changing its intended rules?

## Validation questions

For every apparent improvement I tried to ask:

- How many observations support it?
- Was the rule discovered and tested on the same sample?
- Does it survive chronological splitting?
- Does it survive transaction costs?
- Does it persist across years?
- Does it still work when sample size becomes larger?
- Is the improvement economically meaningful, or only statistically interesting?
